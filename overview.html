Why it exists

The Merrill's Foveon sensor records red, green and blue in three stacked layers at every pixel. SIGMA Photo Pro is still the best renderer for these files, but it is a slow editor with few tools. Camera Raw is a great editor, but its own handling of Merrill files does not look like SPP.

X3FM Converter combines the two. It does not imitate SPP's look: it has SPP's own processing engine render each file, then turns that result back into a raw DNG that Camera Raw can edit freely.

How a file is converted

1 · read

X3F

Raw sensor data, the camera's white-balance preset and any edits saved in SPP.

2 · render

SPP engine

SIGMA Photo Pro's installed engine renders the image: colour, noise reduction, sharpening, highlight repair.

3 · recover

Linear DNG

The render is converted back to linear raw data with SPP's tone curve and colour profiles embedded.

4 · edit

Camera Raw

Opens looking like SPP, with every Camera Raw control available, including extra highlight headroom.

About 5 seconds per file, or about 3.5 seconds per file when converting a batch two at a time.

Features

The look
  • SPP Standard by defaultThe same colour, tone, noise reduction and sharpening SPP gives you out of the box.
  • All SPP colour modes as profilesVivid, Neutral, Portrait, Landscape and FOV Classic Blue in Camera Raw's Profile menu, switchable without reconverting.
  • Monochrome modeSPP's Foveon black and white: its layer mix, monochrome noise reduction and tone curve. Opens in Camera Raw's Black & White mode.
  • White balance as shotEvery camera preset converts with SPP's colour matrix for that light, not just a temperature shift.
  • Your SPP editsFiles edited and saved in SPP convert with those edits: exposure, contrast, shadow, highlight, saturation, sharpness, fill light, noise reduction and colour mode.
  • Advanced settingsHighlight Control (Neutralize to Restore), X3 Fill Light, Sharpness, and SPP's five-step Chroma and Luminance noise reduction.
The workflow
  • Double-click to Camera RawDouble-click an X3F in Finder: it converts in the background and opens in Camera Raw, with no window.
  • Batch windowDrop files or folders, tick the ones you want and convert. Files with SPP edits are tagged.
  • Skips what is doneAlready converted with the same settings? Skipped. Change a setting and it converts again.
  • Correct Finder previewsThumbnails and Quick Look show the real look, not a flat, washed-out version.
  • Highlight headroomCamera Raw can still recover detail in highlights that SPP clips to white.
  • Compact filesLossless compressed DNGs of about 54 MB, saved next to the original or in a folder you choose.

Getting started

  1. Install SIGMA Photo Pro 6 in your Applications folder if it isn't there already. The converter uses its engine and does not work without it.
  2. Unzip the package and copy X3FM Converter and X3FM to Camera Raw into your Applications folder.
  3. Open X3FM Converter with right-click › Open the first time. The apps are not signed by a registered Apple developer, so macOS asks once.
  4. Click Use for double-click at the bottom of the window to make double-clicked X3F files open straight in Camera Raw.
Good to know

Works with the DP Merrill cameras. SPP edits to monochrome settings, the tone-curve editor, crop and lens corrections are not read yet. Saving DNGs to a Mac folder is about twice as fast as writing them back to the SD card.
